How to play

This is what it looks like on your phone. Nora will talk you through it.

1Join

Scan the QR code or open the link, type your name, tap Join the game. No app, no sign-in.

2Wait for the room

You'll see You are in. Keep an eye on the shared screen while everyone joins.

3Fill in the blank

Nora reads a question with a blank. Tap the funniest of your five cards, then Play this card. Don't like your hand? Swap it, twice per game.

4Nora reads them out

Once everyone has played, the answers appear on the big screen and Nora reads each one.

5Vote

Pick your favourite and Lock in this vote. Your own card is greyed out, so no voting for yourself.

  • Most votes wins the round and a point. A tie? Nora picks.
  • The card you played is replaced right away, so you always hold five.
  • You'll never be dealt the same card twice in a game.
  • You can swap your whole hand twice per game, before you've played a card that round.
  • The game runs a set number of rounds. Highest score at the end takes it.
  • You get 30 seconds to play a card and 30 seconds to vote. When the clock runs out the round moves on without you.
  • Wandered off? The host can also close a round early, so nobody waits on one phone.
